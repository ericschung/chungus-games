For grown-ups: putting a game on the iPad's Home Screen
  1. Open the game in Safari (tap its card).
  2. Tap the Share button, then Add to Home Screen, then Add.
  3. The game gets its own icon and opens full screen from it. Each game saves its progress on that iPad.

Chungus HD is the most detailed and needs the most memory. If it is slow or the page reloads on an older iPad, play Chungus 2: it's the same adventure with simpler graphics.
