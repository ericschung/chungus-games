For grown-ups: putting a game on the iPad's Home Screen
  1. Open the game in Safari (tap its card).
  2. Tap the Share button, then Add to Home Screen, then Add.
  3. The game gets its own icon and opens full screen from it. Each game saves its progress on that iPad.

Leo's Whisker Rally is made for about 15 minutes a day: each day is a stop on the rally with a few short games and a drive, and when they are done the team drives on to the next stop (21 in all, with a cup race at the end of each land). He can play any game from All games too. Press and hold the gear in its top corner for the grown-ups page with his progress and ideas to try together away from the screen.

Leo's Reef Crew is made for about 10–15 minutes a day: when all of the day's jobs are done, the crew adds a new building to Reef Town (21 in all). Press and hold the gear in its top corner for the grown-ups page with his progress and a sound check.

Chungus HD is the most detailed and needs the most memory. If it is slow or the page reloads on an older iPad, play Chungus 2: it's the same adventure with simpler graphics.
