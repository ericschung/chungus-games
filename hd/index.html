Leo & Amelia and the Wish Gem

Queen Glimmer the Wish Fairy has found a magic gem that can turn Leo and Amelia into fairies, mermaids and dragons. Fly over the islands, swim down to the Mermaid Palace and light up Dragon Peak to grant three magical wishes.

WASD move1–4 change shapeF magicSpace jump / fly upC fly down / dive

How to play

  • Move with WASD or the arrow keys, hold Shift to go faster, drag to turn the camera and scroll to zoom. On a tablet or phone, drag on the left side to move and on the right side to look.
  • Change shape with the Wish Gem buttons at the bottom, or press 1 kids, 2 fairies, 3 mermaids, 4 dragons. Q picks the next shape.
  • Fairies and dragons fly. Press Space (or the up arrow button) to take off and hold it to go higher, hold C (or the down button) to come down, and let go of both to hover.
  • Mermaids swim. Jump into the sea and the gem turns you into mermaids. Hold C to dive and Space to swim up. At the surface, Space does a dolphin leap.
  • Magic: press F (or the big round button). Fairies sprinkle fairy dust, mermaids sing a bubble song and dragons breathe fire.
  • The three wishes: bloom the five Moonflowers with fairy dust, find Queen Nerissa's five pearls in the giant clams and bring them to the Mermaid Palace, and light the three beacons on Dragon Peak with dragon fire.
  • Stuck? Tap the sparkle button (or press N) and Queen Glimmer will show you the way.

Every wish came true!

The Moonflowers bloom, Queen Nerissa has all her pearls and Dragon Peak shines again. The Wish Gem has one more gift: Leo and Amelia can now turn into unicorns! Press 5 or tap the new unicorn button.
