Leo & Amelia and the Shadow Goddess

Gloomia the Shadow Goddess wants the Wish Gem, and when she gets mad she turns Leo and Amelia into ghosts! Find the Star Crystal and the Sun Crystal on the new floating isles and in the Crystal Caves, uncover her secret lair and catch her in the Starlight Necklace.

How to play

  • Move with WASD or the arrow keys, hold Shift to go faster, drag to turn the camera and scroll to zoom. On a tablet or phone, drag on the left side to move and on the right side to look.
  • Change shape with the Wish Gem buttons at the bottom, or press 1 kids, 2 fairies, 3 mermaids, 4 dragons, 5 unicorns. Q picks the next shape.
  • Fairies and dragons fly. Press Space (or the up arrow button) to take off and hold it to go higher, hold C (or the down button) to come down, and let go of both to hover.
  • Mermaids swim. Jump into the sea and the gem turns you into mermaids. Hold C to dive and Space to swim up. At the surface, Space does a dolphin leap.
  • Magic: press F (or the big round button). Fairies sprinkle fairy dust, mermaids sing a bubble song and dragons breathe fire. Once Leo has the Star Crystal he shoots magic stars, and once Amelia has the Sun Crystal her sunshine shield blocks Gloomia's ghost spells.
  • The adventure: the Star Crystal is in the Star Temple, at the top of the new floating isles (bounce up the giant flowers, or fly). The Sun Crystal is deep in the Crystal Caves on Echo Rock. Then find Gloomia's secret lair, pop her shadow orbs and catch her in the Starlight Necklace.
  • Ghosts: when Gloomia gets mad she turns you into ghosts. Don't worry, it wears off in a few seconds!
  • Stuck? Tap the sparkle button (or press N) and Queen Glimmer will show you the way.

Gloomia is caught!

The Shadow Goddess is trapped inside the Starlight Necklace, and the Wish Gem is safe. Whoever wears the necklace becomes a goddess: Leo and Amelia can now fly as goddesses and use Gloomia's ghost magic. Press 6 or tap the new goddess button.
