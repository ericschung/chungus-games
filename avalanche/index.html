Avalanche Roll

Steer a snowball down a mountain that never ends. It gets bigger the farther it rolls.

  • Drag left and right anywhere to steer. Arrow keys work too.
  • Roll over penguins, puppies, polar bears and snowmen to grow and score.
  • Pass between the flags for a bonus. Hit a ramp to catch birds in the air.
  • Trees, rocks and cabins knock snow off. Ponds and crevasses end the run.
